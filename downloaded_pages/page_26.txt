"Paradise Lost" by John Milton is an epic poem composed in the late 17th century. The narrative explores the theological themes of creation, free will, and the fall of man, focusing particularly on the figures of Satan, Adam, and Eve. The poem examines the consequences of disobedience to God and reflects on the nature of evil, temptation, and redemption.
